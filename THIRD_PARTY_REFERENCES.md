The Chapter 16 reference is F. Hipólito, Alireza Taghizadeh and T. G. Pedersen, “Nonlinear optical response of doped monolayer and bilayer graphene: Length gauge tight-binding model,” Physical Review B 98, 205420 (2018), DOI https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.98.205420, Fig. 3(a), the published mu=0.35 eV curve.
